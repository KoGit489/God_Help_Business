# CASAI Draft 2: 360 Capture Quick Guide

The simple process: make a project, upload its floor plan, attach each 360 photo to its location, and check the route.

## 1. Gather what you need

- Your CASAI email and password.
- A charged 360 camera and memory-card space.
- The correct floor plan for the building and floor.
- A full, stitched panoramic JPG for each capture, exported with the camera manufacturer's software.
- A note matching each photo file to its location and route order.

Keep original camera files as backups. Native `.insp` or `.insv` files are optional; start with panoramic JPGs so you can confirm each image displays properly.

## 2. Choose your route

Decide the order a reviewer should visit locations. For your first try, use three points: **entrance -> corridor -> turn**.

## 3. Sign in and open your project

1. Go to `https://casaifield.com/login.html` and sign in with your individual account.
2. On the Dashboard, create a clearly named project, or use the project prepared for you.
3. Click **Open floor plan walkthrough**.

## 4. Load the floor plan

1. Select the correct project from the **Project** dropdown.
2. If the plan is not visible, click **Upload floor plan**, choose the correct PDF, JPG, or PNG, and wait for it to appear.
3. Enter the width in feet represented by the **entire plan image** in **Plan width (feet)**. Do not automatically accept the default.
4. Confirm the drawing matches the correct building, floor, and revision.

## 5. Capture and save each waypoint

Repeat these steps at each location, in route order:

1. Go to the location and stop somewhere safe.
2. Keep the 360 camera still and take a full 360 photo.
3. Export the capture as a stitched panoramic JPG.
4. In the app, click the camera's location on the plan.
5. Set **Heading** for the route direction on the drawing: `0°` up, `90°` right, `180°` down, `270°` left.
6. Under **Preview image**, choose the matching panoramic JPG.
7. Click **Save waypoint here** once. Wait for the save message; confirm the dot and numbered waypoint appear.
8. Click **View** and check that the correct photo opens.

Choose the next photo file for every waypoint. The file field clears after saving.

## 6. Check the walkthrough

- Open the first waypoint. Click the yellow arrow to advance and the white arrow to go back.
- Check that each arrow leads to the correct next or previous location.
- Open a middle waypoint using both **View** and its map dot; check its direction.
- Drag the image to look around. Confirm it is a complete 360 image, not a crop or placeholder.

**Important:** **Walk one step** only moves the marker on the drawing. It does not move the camera, track your physical walk, or take a photo. For the first walkthrough, click each actual camera location directly on the plan.

## 7. Finish

1. Confirm waypoint count and order match your route notes.
2. Refresh the page and reopen the project. Confirm the plan and waypoints are still there.
3. Recheck **Plan width (feet)**; that setting may need to be entered again.
4. Keep backup copies of your original camera files and panoramic JPGs.
5. Click **Account**, then **Sign out**, especially on a shared device.

## If something goes wrong

- **Plan is blank:** Check the selected project. If it is still blank, upload the correct plan again.
- **Photo is missing:** Select the file under **Preview image**, then click **Save waypoint here** and wait.
- **Wrong photo:** Click **Edit** beside that waypoint, replace the photo, and save.
- **Arrow points the wrong way:** Stop and verify the photo, its map location, and the route order before continuing.
- **Upload fails:** Do not keep clicking Save. Check whether a waypoint was created, then ask the project administrator for help.
